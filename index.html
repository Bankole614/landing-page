Engineered for zero latency and real feel

Modern web standards and game engineering come together to give the browser an authentic Grand Prix experience.

Sub-stepped physics integrator

Semi-implicit Euler integration runs inside a fixed 60 Hz accumulator. Above 340 km/h, each tick subdivides into continuous raycast tests, so cars never phase through barriers.

Tyre wear and weather

Non-linear Pacejka grip fades as rubber wears, costing up to 55% of peak cornering grip. Intermediate and wet stops become real strategy calls.

Worn tyre: 45% grip left

Web Audio synthesizer

No recorded audio. Multi-harmonic FM oscillators follow engine RPM, with low-pass wind filters and band-pass tyre scrub.

Slipstream and DRS

Sit inside a 120 m wake cone behind a rival and drag drops by up to 22%. Open the rear wing on a DRS straight for another 28% off the top end.

Tow −22% drag
DRS −28% drag

Bezier track architect

Design circuits in the browser. Place spline points, check curvature to set AI braking zones, and export the track as portable JSON.

Installable on mobile

A service worker caches the game offline. A virtual steering joystick with haptic feedback and fullscreen launch work on iOS and Android.
